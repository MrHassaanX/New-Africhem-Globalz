## Design decision notes

- Main's strongest contribution is motion quality: Home intro, card overlap, stacked Products cards and interactive globe. 6doc-update's strongest contribution is business clarity: chemical taxonomy, cautious claims, proof, quote flow, About order care and buyer FAQ. The personal version keeps both.
- The 24 October Outstanding Changes Audit evaluates an older snapshot. Its status labels were rechecked against this worktree; they were not copied as current proof.
- Supplier tiers, ten-year experience, guaranteed delivery/COA checking, operational facilities, certificates, specifications, terms and shipping lanes are not asserted as verified facts.
