## Visual asset provenance

The supplied transparent AFRICHEM GLOBALZ logo is unchanged at public/africhem-logo.png. The official mark is cropped only for app/icon.png.

The four WebP material studies in public/materials/ were generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then resized and compressed locally. One source image was a three-panel contact sheet cropped into liquid-glass.webp, mineral-crystal.webp and industrial-fluid.webp. The second source yielded hero-fluid.webp. They are illustrative, not products or stock photographs.

Final imagegen prompts:

1. “Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: three visual panels for a premium chemical sourcing website's editorial product teaser. Generate one seamless-width contact sheet with three DISTINCT equal vertical panels, each a standalone abstract macro material photograph with no borders or margins: LEFT translucent folded liquid glass film with cool navy blue and cyan rim light; CENTER pale mineral crystalline lamellae and prismatic refractions on warm cream; RIGHT brushed industrial steel meeting a deep blue viscous liquid surface, restrained teal highlights. Sophisticated physical textures, high-end scientific material photography, precise light and natural imperfections. Each panel should read well cropped to a 4:5 portrait. No text, no logo, no labels, no bottles, no flasks, no molecule spheres, no people, no bright green, no sci-fi starfield, no UI cards. The result is atmospheric material artwork, not evidence of specific catalogue products.”
2. “Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: abstract catalogue material study. Premium macro photograph of iridescent glassy liquid layers flowing over brushed industrial alloy, cool navy and cyan with warm mineral reflected light, high-end chemical material science photography. Strong sculptural texture, no object packaging. No text, no logo, no flasks, no green, no space or network motifs.”

public/geo/reach-outline.svg and public/geo/reach-outline-light.svg are theme-specific renderings generated by qa/generate-reach-map.cjs from the world-atlas v2.0.2 land-110m dataset, a redistribution of Natural Earth geographic outlines. They are static Home teasers only. No source-archive map asset was copied.
