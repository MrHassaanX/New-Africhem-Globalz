# Legal and measurement review draft

Public `/privacy` and `/terms` explicitly remain review drafts. The final policy requires legal entity, enquiry-processing purpose and provider, storage/retention/deletion details, contact channel and any applicable jurisdictions. Terms of Sale require confirmed payment conditions, Incoterms, quote validity, delivery assumptions, claims/off-spec process, safety/transport-document handling and governing terms. No clause should be represented as approved until the founder and legal reviewer sign it off.

No analytics or advertising tracker is being added. After privacy and form approval, agree a baseline and reporting method for monthly quote requests, quote form starts and completions, technical document downloads and time to first response. Prefer server/provider events and minimal retention. Do not record message content in analytics.

Launch work remains separate: confirm domain, DNS, SSL, publication approval, indexability and Google Business Profile eligibility. `SITE_INDEXABLE` remains false for this review.
